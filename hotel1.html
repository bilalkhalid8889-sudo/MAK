About MAK Hotel

MAK Hotel is a hotel management system developed as a university project. It is designed to streamline hotel operations including reservations, guest management, billing, housekeeping, and reporting — all from a single, easy-to-use dashboard.

About This Project

MAK Hotel Management System is a university project built to simulate a real-world hotel management platform. The system covers all core hotel operations — room availability, guest check-ins and check-outs, billing, housekeeping, and reporting — accessible from a single dashboard.

The project was developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with AI-assisted development as part of the workflow. The team focused on defining requirements, making design decisions, testing functionality, and ensuring the system meets practical, real-world standards. The name MAK is formed from the initials of the three team members.
